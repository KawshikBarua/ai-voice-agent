01  The problem we solve

Every missed call is a lost customer. For appointment-based businesses, the phone is still the front door — and it rings when you are least able to answer it.

The cost of a ringing phone

  • You are already busy. Treating a patient, under a sink, or with a client — the phone rings and nobody picks up.
  • Callers do not leave voicemail. Most hang up and call the next business on the list.
  • Calls arrive after hours. Evenings, weekends and holidays are when emergencies happen and when people have time to call.
  • A receptionist is expensive. A full-time hire costs far more than the revenue many small businesses lose to missed calls — and they still go home at 5pm.
  • The same questions, endlessly. "How much is a check-up?" "Are you open Saturday?" "Where are you located?"

The result is quietly expensive: revenue walks away, your calendar has gaps you did not choose, and the time you do spend on the phone is time away from the work you are paid for.

A business that answers every call books more work than one that answers most of them — without anyone working longer hours.

02  What the platform does

Frontly answers your business phone with a natural-sounding voice, holds a real conversation with the caller, and completes the task — then records everything in your dashboard.

On the phone, it can

Book appointments

Checks your real availability, offers open times, and books the appointment while the caller is still on the line.

Cancel and reschedule

Finds the caller's existing booking, confirms their identity, and moves or cancels it.

Quote prices

Reads your actual price list for services and products. It never guesses a number.

Recognise returning customers

Greets people it has spoken to before and pulls up their history.

Answer common questions

Opening hours, location, policies, what you do and do not offer.

Hand over to a human

Transfers the call to you or your team whenever it is unsure or the caller asks.

In your dashboard, you get

  • Every call logged, with a written transcript and a short summary of what happened
  • A live calendar of appointments the AI booked while you were working
  • A customer record for every caller, with their full history in one timeline
  • Figures on revenue booked, payments outstanding and calls handled

It works around your business, not the other way round

You tell the platform your services, prices, opening hours and policies. The AI only ever uses that information — so it cannot invent a price, promise a time you are closed, or offer a service you do not provide.

Safety comes first

For genuine emergencies — a gas smell, a burst pipe flooding a property, exposed live wiring, or a medical crisis — the AI gives the correct immediate safety instruction and routes the call to a person rather than trying to book an appointment.

04  Getting started

Setup takes about twenty minutes. Do it once, then adjust whenever your business changes.

  1. Sign in Use the email address and password provided to you. You will land on your dashboard.
  2. Enter your business details Go to Settings and fill in your business name, industry, address, phone number, currency and time zone. The industry setting is important — it shapes how the AI talks to your callers.
  3. Set your opening hours Still in Settings. The AI will never offer an appointment outside these hours, and will tell callers when you are closed.
  4. Add your services Under Services, list what you offer with a duration and a price range. This is exactly what the AI quotes, so keep it accurate.
  5. Add your products (optional) If you sell parts or items over the phone, add them with prices and stock levels. Businesses that do not sell products can skip this entirely.
  6. Fill in your knowledge base Add your frequently asked questions, policies and anything else callers regularly ask about. See section 10.
  7. Set how many jobs you can run at once In Settings, set the number of staff or rooms available. The AI uses this so it never double-books you.
  8. Add a transfer number A real phone number the AI can transfer callers to when it needs a human.

Check your work before you go live

Open the Knowledge Base page and choose Preview final AI prompt. This shows you exactly what your AI knows, assembled from everything you entered. If something looks wrong there, it will sound wrong on the phone.

06  Appointments and calendar

Appointments arrive two ways: booked by the AI during a call, or added by you.

The life of an appointment

StatusWhat it means and what to do
ScheduledBooked but not yet confirmed by your team. Review it and press Confirm.
ConfirmedYou have accepted it. You can now record payment or mark it complete.
CompletedThe work is done. The customer's visit count updates automatically.
CancelledCalled off. The time slot is released for other bookings.

Payment can only be recorded after confirmation

The Mark paid button appears once an appointment is confirmed. This prevents money being recorded against a booking nobody has checked yet.

Using the calendar

The calendar shows the month with a dot on every day that has bookings. Click any day to see what is scheduled, who it is for and its current status. Use the arrows to move between months.

Adding an appointment yourself

Press + New Appointment, choose the customer and service, pick a date and time, and save. If the slot is already full you will be told immediately — the platform will not let you double-book.

07  Customers

Every caller becomes a customer record. You do not have to create them by hand — when the AI books an appointment for somebody new, their record is created during the call.

Recognising people you already know

When someone calls, the platform looks them up by phone number first, then email, then name. If they are an existing customer, the AI knows their history and greets them accordingly. Phone numbers are matched regardless of how they are written, so (555) 010-0888 and +15550100888 are treated as the same person.

The customer timeline

Click any customer to expand their record. You will see when they first came to you, their last visit, how many times they have visited, and a complete timeline of everything that has happened — calls, bookings, cancellations, completed work and payments — with the date and whether it was done by the AI or by a member of your team.

Removing a customer

Deleting a customer hides them from your lists but preserves their history for your records. Nothing is permanently destroyed.

08  Call history

The Calls page is the record of every conversation your AI has had.

Each entry shows who called, when, and how long it lasted. Open one and you get:

  • A summary — a few lines describing what the caller wanted and what happened
  • The full transcript — the complete conversation in writing
  • The recording — play back the call where available

Calls are linked to the customer automatically where the phone number matches, so a caller's conversations also appear on their timeline.

If a call seems to be missing

Press Sync calls at the top of the Calls page. This re-checks the phone service for any conversations that have not yet appeared and imports them, along with their transcripts and summaries.

Transcripts and summaries are a useful record for training, for resolving disputes about what was agreed, and for spotting questions your knowledge base should answer.

10  Teaching your AI about your business

The Knowledge Base is where you put everything a good receptionist would know. The more you put in, the fewer calls need a human.

CategoryWhat to put there
Business infoWhat you do, how long you have been going, parking, accessibility, languages spoken
FAQThe questions you answer every week: do you take walk-ins, do you take card, do I need to bring anything
PolicyCancellation notice, deposits, late arrivals, guarantees, payment terms
Emergency rulesExactly what the AI should say and do when a call is urgent
CustomAnything else that does not fit above

Writing good entries

  • Write the way you would say it out loud — the AI is speaking, not printing a brochure
  • One subject per entry, with a clear title
  • Be specific: "Cancellations are free up to 12 hours before" beats "please give notice"
  • Review it monthly and add anything callers asked that the AI could not answer

What you cannot change — and why that is good

The core instructions that keep your AI honest are built into the platform and cannot be edited. They are what stop it inventing prices, promising appointments that do not exist, or making up policies. You control all of your business information; the safety rules stay switched on.

A good greeting

"Hello, thank you for calling Springfield Plumbing. I am the AI receptionist for Springfield Plumbing. How may I help you today?"

The AI always identifies itself as an AI. This is deliberate — callers trust a system that is honest about what it is, and in many places it is also a legal requirement.

12  Team roles and permissions

Each member of your team gets a role that decides what they can do.

RoleCan do
Organisation adminEverything: business settings, team, services, products, knowledge base, appointments and customers
ManagerEverything except organisation-level settings — runs the day-to-day and maintains the catalogue and knowledge base
ReceptionistBooks, confirms, reschedules and cancels appointments; creates and edits customers
Read onlyCan view everything but change nothing — useful for accountants and reporting

If a button does not work for you, your role most likely does not permit that action; the platform will tell you when this happens. Ask your administrator to change your role.

Your data stays yours

Your business is completely separated from every other business on the platform. No other organisation can see your customers, appointments, prices or calls. Every significant action is recorded in an audit trail that your team cannot delete.

13  Frequently asked questions

Will callers know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. It introduces itself as an AI receptionist at the start of every call. Callers who want a person can ask at any point and will be transferred.

What if the AI does not know the answer?

It says so and offers to transfer the caller or take a message. It is built never to guess. If you see the same question coming up in transcripts, add it to your knowledge base.

Can it give a wrong price?

It can only quote what you entered under Services and Products. If a price is wrong on the phone, it is wrong in your settings — update it and the change takes effect immediately.

What happens outside opening hours?

It still answers. It will tell the caller you are closed and offer the next available appointment, so you wake up to a booked diary instead of missed calls.

Can it double-book me?

No. It checks live availability before offering any time, and the booking is checked again at the moment it is made — so even two callers on the line at once cannot take the same slot.

What if somebody calls to cancel someone else's appointment?

The AI asks for the caller's name and checks it against the booking before cancelling or rescheduling. If it does not match, it refuses and passes the call to a human.

Do I keep my existing phone number?

Yes. Most businesses keep their advertised number and forward it to the AI, so nothing changes for your customers.

How do I turn it off?

Stop forwarding your calls, or ask your administrator to disable the agent. Your dashboard, customers and history remain exactly as they are.

Need help?

Contact your platform administrator or your account manager. Have your business name and, where relevant, the date and time of the call in question — every call is logged with a transcript, which usually answers the question straight away.
